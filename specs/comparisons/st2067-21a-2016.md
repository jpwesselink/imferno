## Summary
- 2 XSD constructs inventoried (`MaxCLL`, `MaxFALL` — both HDR static metadata)
- ~30 prose-only constraints (App #2E is dominated by image-essence and color-system prose rules)
- 0 conflicts
- The XSD is **tiny by design**. App #2E layers on top of App #2 (ST 2067-20) + Core Constraints (ST 2067-2) and only adds the two HDR metadata elements as schema; everything else is prose.

## Catalogue coverage cross-check

The 2016 edition itself maps to `St2067_21_2020::AppIdMismatch` for
the §7.1 ApplicationIdentification check (the App #2E 2020 catalogue
URI; for 2016 you'd compare against the 2016 URI literal).

Most of the 2016 catalogue work in the engine actually targets the
**2023 edition** of App #2E (`St2067_21_2023` enum, ~70 codes) which
extends 2016 with UHD/HDR-specific tightening. Coverage for the

catalogue, which targets a superset of the 2016 constraints. The
2016 edition's prose is largely covered indirectly when validating
2016-era CPLs against the 2023 catalogue.
